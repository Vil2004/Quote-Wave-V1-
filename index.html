What it tracks

Every angle on the last digit.

The same patterns that show up in match/differ, rise/fall, over/under, and even/odd contracts — computed live, not after the fact.

01

Rise / Fall streaks

Live direction tracking with current and longest streak counters, so momentum runs are obvious at a glance.

02

Even / Odd split

03

Getting started

Three steps to a live dashboard.

01

Grab an App ID

Use Deriv's public test ID (1089) to start instantly, or register your own at developers.deriv.com.

02

Optionally add an API token

Create a read-scope token in your Deriv account settings to authorize and sync your real trade history.

03

Connect and watch

Pick a symbol, hit connect, and the tape starts streaming — stats update with every tick.

Quotewave is an analysis tool, not financial advice. It surfaces statistical patterns in publicly streamed tick data — it does not predict outcomes, and past digit or price behavior does not determine future ticks. Trading synthetic indices carries real risk of loss. Trade log data is stored locally in your browser or device; Quotewave does not place trades on your behalf.
